# Business stakeholders

**Who they are:** Product, finance, operations, sales and other business leads who own outcomes the work affects but aren't hands-on technically.

**What they care about:** What it means for their goals: cost, timeline, risk and customer impact. They want to know what they need to decide or do.

**What they'd ask:**
- What does it cost, and when will it be done?
- What are the risks, and what happens if we don't do it?
- What do you need from me?
- How will we know it worked?

**What loses them:** Technical detail beyond what they need, jargon, and a presentation with no clear ask.

**How they react:** They ask for the bottom line early, and when you explain the how, they steer you back to impact and timing.
