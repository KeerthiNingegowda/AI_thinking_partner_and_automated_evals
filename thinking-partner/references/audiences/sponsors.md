# Sponsors

**Who they are:** Business leaders who fund or champion the work. They aren't technical themselves, but they want to see technical results.

**What they care about:** Proof it's working and worth the investment: progress against the plan, measurable results, and whether they can trust the numbers. They need to be able to explain it to others.

**What they'd ask:**
- What have we got for the money and time so far?
- What do these numbers mean in plain terms? Is that good?
- How do you know the results are real?
- What's next, what could go wrong, and do you need anything more from me?

**If the work is data science or ML:**
- What data is this based on, and is it good enough?
- How does the model reach its answers, in plain terms? Why did it say that?
- How often is it wrong, and what happens when it is?
- Can we trust it to support this decision?

**What loses them:** Raw metrics with no meaning attached, technical detail with no "so what," a presentation that is all good news with no mention of problems, and being asked to take the model on trust, as a black box.

**How they react:** They ask you to translate a number into impact, and they check that the results are solid before they repeat them to others.
