# Technical audience

**Who they are:** Engineers, data scientists, researchers, architects and others who build, run or study the thing being discussed.

**What they care about:** Whether it's correct, how it works, and how it fails. They want specifics: numbers, constraints and trade-offs.

**What they'd ask:**
- How does it work, and why this approach over the alternatives?
- What are the edge cases and failure modes?
- How does it scale, and what does it cost to run and maintain?
- How do you know? What's the evidence?
- Is the method sound, and how does it compare to prior work?

**What loses them:** Vague claims, skipping past the hard part, business jargon standing in for detail, and being told it "just works."

**How they react:** They interrupt to probe a specific claim and stay on a detail until it's answered.
