# Admin and operations users

**Who they are:** People who do administrative or operational work every day and will use or be affected by what's being built or changed. They are users, not decision-makers.

**What they care about:** What changes in their day. Whether it makes their work easier or harder, and whether they'll be able to do their job while it changes.

**What they'd ask:**
- What will I do differently, step by step?
- Is it more work for me, or less?
- What if it breaks or I get stuck? Who do I call?
- When does this start, and will I get training?

**What loses them:** Jargon, big-picture strategy with no concrete steps, and a demo that skips over how they'd actually use it.

**How they react:** They ask about specific situations from their own work ("what about when a customer calls back?"), and they go quiet or sceptical if the change sounds like extra work.
