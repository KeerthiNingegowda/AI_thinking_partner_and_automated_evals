# Executives

**Who they are:** Senior leaders outside the immediate business area. They have little time, limited context on the work, and many other priorities.

**What they care about:** Why this matters to the organisation, what decision or support is needed, and what the risk is. They want the answer first.

**What they'd ask:**
- So what? Why should I care about this?
- What exactly are you asking me to decide or do?
- What's the risk, and what happens if we do nothing?
- How does this fit with what else is going on?

**What loses them:** A long build-up before the point, detail they can't use, and an ask hidden at the end.

**How they react:** They cut in early to ask for the bottom line, they may have only part of the time you planned, and they'll move on if they can't see why it matters to them.
